CSCD: Vietnam Mobile Police is currently in development — there's no public build yet. The fastest way to follow progress and get early access is to:

1. Wishlist the game on its [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1886820/CSCD_Vietnam_Mobile_Police/) to get notified as soon as a demo or early access build goes live.
2. Join the [Discord](https://dsc.gg/cscdvmp) to keep up with closed playtests — the team usually invites a small group of players to try each major update before it ships.

## Recommended specs

Official specs will be announced closer to release, once the engine is more stable. This page will be updated as soon as that information is available.
