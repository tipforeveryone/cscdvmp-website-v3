> **Note:** Some features and UI elements mentioned in this guide may not be functional yet.

## 1. Create Profile

- The first time you start the game, after agreeing to the Terms of Service, you'll be asked to create a profile. This profile will save all the details of the team you'll be managing.
- Afterwards, simply choose your preferred playstyle.

![Create Profile](https://i.gyazo.com/85a8ce4aff9635728268b8265d6187db.gif)

---

## 2. Recruitment, Acquiring Weapons and Attachments

**Recruitment**

- Select operators by clicking their credit cost button. Click once more to deselect.
- Players can command up to 3 squads, each with a maximum of 5 operators.
- If the current candidate list isn't suitable, click **Refresh** for a new one.
- Important: At present, soldier skill ratings have no impact on gameplay.

**Acquiring Weapons and Attachments**

- You need to select enough weapons to fully equip your chosen operators. Each operator can carry 1 primary weapon (like an assault rifle, SMG, or sniper rifle) and 1 secondary weapon (sidearm).
- Similarly, ensure the selected attachments are sufficient for your chosen weapons. Each weapon can be equipped with multiple attachments.

![Recruitment](https://i.gyazo.com/fb967bfafbcc223f50fb1e5adfb3ef94.gif)

- You can use the Auto-pick feature to have the game make selections for you. This saves time, but it doesn't guarantee optimal results for your squad.

![Auto-pick](https://i.gyazo.com/c6a7c7f72fb2fe8331947120d75ace6a.gif)

---

## 3. Finish

- Once you're happy with your choices, click **Confirm** and then **Yes** to finalize everything.
- The game will then take you to the main menu screen. Congratulations, you've successfully completed the initial profile setup for your first time in the game!

---

## 4. Pre-made Profile

- If you prefer not to create a profile, or if your profile creation fails, you can download a pre-made profile file provided by the development team below.

**[Download 'profile1.dat'](https://transfer.it/t/m4YTqM0lWBTA)**

- This profile includes optimized choices for personnel, weaponry, and attachments, as well as the setup of operators within one attack squad. Just copy it to the **Documents/cscdvmp/profiles** directory, overwriting existing files if necessary.
- You may also rename the file to **profile2.dat** or **profile3.dat**. It will then be displayed in the respective slot in the in-game profile list.
