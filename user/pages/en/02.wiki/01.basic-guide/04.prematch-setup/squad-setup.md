> **Note:** Some features and UI elements mentioned in this guide may not be functional yet.

## 1. Map Selection

- Click **Play** to open map list then select map you want to play.
- Each map offers up to 3 starting positions. Click the illustration to view its location on the map to the right.
- Select your squad's spawn point by clicking on numbers 1, 2, 3 below.
- **Minimap:** Scroll your mouse wheel to zoom in/out. Hold and drag the Right Mouse Button to pan the view.

![Map Selection](https://i.gyazo.com/b8be52dbd6d32cad113c2a17a09773bb.gif)

---

## 2. Organizing Squads

- First, select your desired squad slot.
- From the soldier list, click **Select** to add the corresponding soldier to the selected slot.

![Organizing Squads](https://i.gyazo.com/3742a335baa9328965c3c847c5bd158f.gif)

---

## 3. Assign Weapons

- Click each soldier's weapon slots to select suitable weapons.

![Assign Weapons](https://i.gyazo.com/2a0aa679f49425294e18a68c19ebe2c5.gif)

- To view a soldier's detailed information and perform similar operations, click on their portrait.

![Soldier Details](https://i.gyazo.com/59816d8e1143233148926329bbecdf5a.gif)

---

## 4. Customize Weapon

- To configure a weapon, click its corresponding gear icon.
- Select an attachment from the list by clicking on the designated attachment points.
- The position of attachments can be adjusted on certain points, potentially having a noticeable effect on the weapon's handling and overall shooting experience in the game.

![Customize Weapon](https://i.gyazo.com/4dbcce5da4bad880fb2fcf9946d77379.gif)

---

## 5. Select Starting Points

- Once you have your desired squad, return to the map information screen and select the starting positions for each team.
- Click **Start** to begin the match!

![Select Starting Points](https://i.gyazo.com/f143c5c34411292c59620197279ddde0.gif)
