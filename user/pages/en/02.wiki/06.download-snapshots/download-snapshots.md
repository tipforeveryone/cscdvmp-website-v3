Snapshots are versions generated after short development cycles, containing bug fixes and new features. By accessing these snapshots, players can experience the game in its active development phase firsthand.

It's important to understand that snapshots are early development versions of the game. They are not the finished product, consequently, you should anticipate finding various bugs, unexpected issues, and features that are not yet fully implemented.

Newer snapshots inherently include all features and improvements from older ones.

For detailed setup and launch instructions, see the guide at [Snapshots Settings](/en/wiki/basic-guide/snapshots-settings).
