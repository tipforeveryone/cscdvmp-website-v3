**Date:** April 17, 2026

## New Features

The update introduces several gameplay enhancements:

- **Visual Effects Enhancement**: Developers added sophisticated impact animations for various materials including concrete, metal, and electrical objects. Gun muzzle smoke effects now dynamically render when firing.

- **Rear-Support Mechanic**: A new system allows players to reposition support NPCs to either their left or right side during movement, maintaining formation cohesion.

- **Formation Behavior**: The CSCD formation system now has the "leader fall-back" feature where units retreat together, preventing player movement obstruction.

## Improvements and Fixes

The patch addresses multiple technical and gameplay elements:

- Character audio ceases when NPCs are eliminated
- Bullet damage values adjusted for hardcore and realistic gameplay modes
- NPC shooting accuracy improved through combat mechanics refinement
- Memory efficiency enhanced via optimized asset preloading
- Animation library management system overhauled for character animation workflows
- Laser beam visibility improved with brighter surface rendering
- Bug fix for maps lacking background music
