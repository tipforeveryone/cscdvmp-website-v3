After months of internal testing, the Legion Team wants to share what's next for CSCD: Vietnam Mobile Police.

Over the coming stretch, we're focusing on polishing the enemy AI system, expanding the roster of urban maps inspired by real Vietnamese settings, and optimizing performance for mid-range devices. Alongside that, closed playtests on our Discord will run more frequently so we can gather direct feedback from the community.

Thank you for being with us since the earliest days of this project. Keep an eye on this blog so you don't miss an update!
