One of the things that really shapes CSCD's tactical feel is how enemies react to what the player does.

Instead of moving through a fixed script, the AI system evaluates line of sight, noise, and cover in real time — enemies can spread out to flank, call for backup, or fall back when they're outmatched. The goal is for every approach to demand actual observation and decision-making rather than memorizing a pattern.

We'll keep tuning the difficulty curve and share more technical details in future posts.
