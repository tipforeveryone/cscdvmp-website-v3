CSCD's new urban map draws on familiar Vietnamese streetscapes — tightly packed tube houses, a maze of narrow alleys, and crowded markets.

Our environment team spent weeks studying real-world layouts to keep the space both recognizable and genuinely useful for tactical play: multiple approach routes, elevated vantage points, and sections that can be partially destroyed to open up new paths mid-match.

This map will show up in our next closed playtest — follow our Discord to find out how to sign up.
