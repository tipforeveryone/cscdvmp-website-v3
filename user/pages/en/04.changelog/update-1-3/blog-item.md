**Date:** April 17, 2026

## New features

- **Advanced visual effects** for bullet impacts on concrete, metal, electrical objects/devices, and dynamic gun muzzle smoke upon firing.

![Bullet impact vfx](https://i.gyazo.com/da42c3c07264b8ad4c1762392844ad5b.gif) ![Gun muzzle smoke](https://i.gyazo.com/e88d62bdac1c0442ea9d6293c0b6f406.gif)

- **The new rear-support mechanic** lets players dynamically switch the support NPC's position to their left or right as they move. This ensures smooth and reliable movement together.

![Rear-support mechanic](https://i.gyazo.com/27b3fa5d2a1670607f16d584cb7db293.gif)

- **New CSCD formation behavior:** When the leader falls back, the whole formation retreats accordingly, preventing any movement obstruction for the player.

![Formation behavior](https://i.gyazo.com/079c06115e5ae148f9ed5f4c7a4d4a8c.gif)

## Improvement and Fix

- Character voice stops upon being killed.
- Rebalance bullet hit damage to adapt hardcore element, especially for realistic playstyle.
- Enhance NPC combat mechanic with better shooting accuracy.
- Optimize asset preload technique for better memory management.
- Revamped Animation Library Management for the character system significantly boosts animation workflow for future mechanics and features.
- Clearer and brighter laser beam surface touch.
- Crash on map which has no in-game music.
