You may want to check whether your (international) document shall be commissioned or notarized.

Many legal documents can be sworn, affirmed or declared before a Commissioner of Oaths. If the document itself is going to be used outside the jurisdiction, especially internationally, you will likely need to swear or declare before a Notary.
