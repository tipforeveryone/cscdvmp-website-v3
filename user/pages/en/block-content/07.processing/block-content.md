It will take you less than 15 minutes to complete your notary service. For a Notary Public to notarize your signature, you must sign it in the physical presence of the Notary.

Generally speaking, it is best to wait until the Notary tells you that you can sign, as you may need to swear an oath first, for example. You simply sign the document again in front of the Notary.
