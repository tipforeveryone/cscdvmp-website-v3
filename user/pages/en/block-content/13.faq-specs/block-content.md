- **Operating System:** 64-bit Windows 10 or later
- **Graphics Card:** DirectX 10 / Shader Model 4.0 compatible (GeForce 8 series, Radeon HD 2000 series, Intel GMA X3500, or newer)
- **Processor:** CPU with SSE2 instruction support
