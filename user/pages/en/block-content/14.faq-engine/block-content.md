We're building our game with the Titan Engine, a creation of Greg Slazinski, formerly known as the Esenthel Engine. Although Titan Engine may not surpass high-end engines like Unreal Engine or Unity in many aspects, it offers unique advantages. These include its ease of use, a streamlined development pipeline, rapid compilation speeds, and a robust IDE. This makes Titan Engine an ideal choice for indie game development.
