Vietnam's completion of the procedures to accede to the **1961 Apostille Convention** marks a significant step toward simplifying the use of documents between countries. Once the Convention officially takes effect, many types of documents—such as academic diplomas, birth certificates, marriage certificates, corporate records, and legal documents—will be able to be used in multiple countries with a single Apostille certification instead of the previous multi-step consular legalization process.

For the Vietnamese community in Canada, as well as individuals and businesses that need to use Canadian documents in Vietnam or other countries, proper notarization, certification, and Apostille of documents are essential steps to ensure that their paperwork is accepted quickly and without complications.
