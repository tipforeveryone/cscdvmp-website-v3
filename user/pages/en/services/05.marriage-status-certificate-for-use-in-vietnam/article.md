The Marriage Status Certificate is issued by the local People's Committee in Vietnam or by Vietnamese diplomatic missions abroad. It confirms an individual's marital status (e.g., single, married, divorced). For Vietnamese citizens living abroad or foreign nationals with ties to Vietnam, this certificate is often required in various situations, including:

- **Marriage Registration in Vietnam:** A prerequisite for completing legal procedures for marriage.
- **Other Legal Matters:** Required for legal disputes, property division, or real estate transactions involving marital status.
